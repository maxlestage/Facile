## What this site does not do

No advertising, no tracker, no third-party analytics, no tracking cookie. Fonts, scripts and styles all come from this site: your browser contacts no other domain to show it.

## The newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address and the language you chose are kept in the site's database, to send you a letter at each release of Facile, and for nothing else. They are neither sold nor shared. To have them deleted, answer any letter or open a request on the repository.

## The server's logs

Like any web server, this one may write in its technical logs the address, the path and the time of requests, for security (rate limits, firewall). These logs serve only that purpose.

## The browser's storage

The demonstration components may keep values in your browser's `localStorage` (a draft, say). They stay on your device.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the data about you, as the GDPR provides.
